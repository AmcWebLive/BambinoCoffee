The little bar on Banksia

Small in size.
Uncompromising in the cup.

Bambino is Ivanhoe's neighbourhood espresso bar — a tiny room built around one big idea: that the best coffee in the area shouldn't require a trip into the city.

Every morning from 6:30, the machine is warm, the cabinet is full of house-baked cakes, pastries and tiny eats, and the first commuters are already at the bench. Pull up a chair out front, bring the dog, stay a while.
